NovaDesk Customer Support - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. NovaDesk offers a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of their first purchase. After 30 days, payments are not refunded, but the subscription can be cancelled at any time and stays active until the end of the paid billing period.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel subscription. Cancellation must be submitted at least 7 days before the renewal date, otherwise the subscription renews for another billing period.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click the Forgot password link on the login page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. Customer account passwords must be at least 10 characters long. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support@novatech.example.

Q: What are the support hours?
A: Starter customers get email support only. Business customers get email and live chat support Sunday to Thursday from 08:00 to 20:00 Cairo time. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone, email and chat support.

Q: How fast does support reply?
A: Target first-response times are 48 hours for Starter, 8 business hours for Business, and 1 hour for critical (P1) issues on Enterprise.

Q: Is there a discount for annual billing?
A: Yes. Paying annually gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. All invoices are issued in US dollars. Credit cards are accepted on all plans, and bank transfer is available for Enterprise plans only.

Q: How do I add more agents to my plan?
A: Admins can add agents at any time from Settings, then Billing. New agents are charged pro rata for the rest of the current billing period.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. Admins can export all tickets, contacts and knowledge base articles in CSV or JSON format from Settings, then Data, then Export. The export file is usually ready within 24 hours. After a subscription is cancelled, your data is kept for 30 days and then permanently deleted.

Q: How can I contact the sales team?
A: Email sales@novatech.example or call extension 4700 during working hours. For technical problems, email support@novatech.example.
